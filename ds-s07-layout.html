Content Containers

Max-width containers that control content measure for readability, app layouts, dashboards, and full-bleed sections.

Prose — 640px
max-width: 640px

Ideal for blog posts, long-form content, and campaign descriptions. Keeps line lengths between 55–75 characters for comfortable reading. Perfect for your Mzansi brand stories.

Standard — 1100px
max-width: 1100px (shown full-width in context)

The default app content container used for post composers, settings panels, and account management views.

Wide — 1400px
max-width: 1400px (shown full-width in context)

For dashboards and analytics views that need room for complex data visualisations and side-by-side panels.

Full Bleed — 100%
Edge to edge — no max-width constraint

Hero banners, media galleries, and immersive visual sections that break free from container limits.
